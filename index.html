About ISLA

ISLA is a healthcare interoperability platform being developed to help patients securely connect, view, and manage their health information in one place.

ISLA is currently preparing for launch.

Medicare Connectivity

ISLA is being developed to support Medicare connectivity, allowing Medicare beneficiaries to authorize access to certain Medicare information through applications they choose to trust.

"This product uses the Blue Button APIs but is not endorsed or certified by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services."
